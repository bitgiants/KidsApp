Parent PIN

Lock these settings and the on/off switch behind a PIN or short passphrase (4–12 characters, no spaces — longer is safer). Kids can't change or disable the extension without it. Setting a PIN also gives you a one-time recovery code in case you forget it.

Screen Time

A daily limit counts minutes of actual playback (paused videos don't count). When it's used up, a friendly "Time's up" screen appears and a parent can add 15 minutes with the PIN. Schedules block YouTube during set hours — homework time, bedtime — with a "taking a break" screen.

Blocked Categories

AI Prompt

Analysis Options

Control what data the AI uses when evaluating videos.

Tells the AI who it is filtering for; younger ranges make it stricter. Substituted wherever the prompt contains {{AGE_RANGE}}.

When enabled, the AI also reads the first ~2 minutes of subtitles for more accurate decisions. Slightly slower but catches misleading titles. Disable for faster checks using title/channel/description only.

Whitelisted Channels

Videos from these channels are never sent to the AI. They still pause for under a second while the extension confirms the channel, so a bad video can't slip through on a stale page. Tick Trusted — play instantly to skip even that for channels you trust completely. Enter an @handle, a channel name, or simply paste the URL of any video or channel page — the @handle is looked up for you.

Recommended Videos & Channels

Shown on every "Video Blocked" screen as "Try one of these instead". Paste a YouTube video URL, a channel URL, a playlist URL, or an @handle, and add an optional friendly label. If this list is empty, whitelisted @channels are offered instead.

Blacklisted Channels

These channels are always blocked — no AI check needed. An @handle, a name, or a pasted video / channel URL.

Recently Blocked

Videos your child tried to open that were blocked, with the reason. Approve a channel or allow a single video with one click — the decision cache clears automatically so the change applies at once.

This Week

Last 7 days: videos allowed and blocked when opened, and minutes watched. All of this stays on this device.
